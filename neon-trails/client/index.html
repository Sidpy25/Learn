How to play

  • Steer your light-cycle with ◀ and ▶. It never stops moving.
  • Every trail is deadly — walls, rivals and your own.
  • Trails have gaps now and then. Slip through them!
  • Each time someone crashes, every survivor scores a point.
  • First to the target score with a 2-point lead wins.
